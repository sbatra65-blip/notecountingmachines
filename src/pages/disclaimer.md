## Fake note detection has limits

Note counting machines with UV, MG, IR, MT or CIS sensors greatly reduce the risk of accepting counterfeit notes, but no machine can guarantee that every fake will be caught. Keep the machine's software updated for new note series, and train staff to check suspect notes by hand using RBI's published security features. We are not responsible for losses from counterfeit notes that a machine fails to detect.
