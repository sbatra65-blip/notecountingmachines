- We acknowledge your complaint **within 48 hours**.

- Please include your name, phone number, invoice number (if any) and a short description of the problem.

## If you are not satisfied

You can approach the National Consumer Helpline (dial 1915, or visit consumerhelpline.gov.in). For complaints about personal data, you can approach the Data Protection Board of India.
