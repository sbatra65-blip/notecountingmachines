This policy applies to machines and accessories bought from **Infinite Solutions** through NoteCountingMachines.com, by phone or by email.

## Return window

You can return a product **within 1 week (7 days) of delivery.**

## Conditions for a refund

The product must come back with **all its original packing, boxes, accessories, manuals and any other material supplied with it, in undamaged condition.**

**If anything is missing or damaged, the refund will be rejected.** Please keep all packing material safe for the first week.

## How to return a product

1. Call **88260 35757**, **88260 35858** or **88260 35959**, or email **notecountingmachines@zohomail.in**, within 7 days of delivery. Give your invoice number and the reason for the return.
2. We will tell you how to send the product back.
3. When it arrives, we inspect the product and all its packing, and tell you whether the refund is approved.

## Refunds

Once your return is approved, we confirm the refund amount and process the refund.

## Faulty machines

If a machine develops a fault, contact us for repair or replacement under warranty. See [Terms of Sale](/terms-of-sale/) for warranty details.
