NoteCountingMachines.com is run by **Infinite Solutions**, 312, Plot No. 4, Vishwadeep Building, Janakpuri District Centre, Janakpuri, New Delhi 110058 (GSTIN 07AFEPB0340P1ZO). In this policy, "we" and "us" mean Infinite Solutions. This policy explains what personal data we collect through this website and how we use it. It follows the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

## What we collect

**When you send an enquiry.** The quote form asks for your name, mobile number, email (optional), city, the type of machine you want and a message. We collect only what you type in.

**When you call or email us.** Your phone number, email address and anything you tell us, so we can answer you.

**When you browse the site.** We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not run ads on this site. Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, processes technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to deliver the site and protect it from attacks. Cloudflare may set a strictly necessary security cookie for this purpose. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when your browser downloads them.

## How we use it

- To reply to your enquiry, send a quotation and arrange a demo.
- To deliver, install, invoice and service a machine you buy from us, including warranty work and software updates.
- To meet legal duties, such as keeping GST invoices and tax records.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for marketing to you unless you ask us to keep you informed.

## How your enquiry reaches us

The enquiry form sends your details by email, through Cloudflare's email service, to our business mailbox (notecountingmachines@zohomail.in, hosted by Zoho). The website itself does not keep a database of enquiries.

## Who we share it with

We share personal data only when needed:

- **Service providers** that run our website and email (Cloudflare and Zoho). They process data on our behalf.
- **Manufacturers or authorised service partners**, when needed to deliver, install or repair a machine you bought.
- **Authorities**, when the law requires it.

## How long we keep it

We keep enquiry emails for as long as needed to deal with your request and any follow-up. If you buy from us, we keep invoices and related records for as long as tax and accounting laws require. After that, we delete the data.

## How we protect it

The whole site runs only over HTTPS (encrypted), with strict security headers. Access to our business mailbox is limited to our staff.

## Your rights

Under the DPDP Act, 2023, you can ask us to:

- tell you what personal data we hold about you and how we use it,
- correct or update it,
- delete it, unless the law requires us to keep it,
- withdraw any consent you gave, and
- nominate another person to exercise these rights for you.

To use any of these rights, or to raise a complaint, email **notecountingmachines@zohomail.in** or call **88260 35757**, **88260 35858** or **88260 35959**. We will reply as soon as possible. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

## Children

This site is meant for businesses and adults buying equipment. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
