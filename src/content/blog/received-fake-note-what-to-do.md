A fake note at the counter is a loss you can't recover, so the aim is to stop it before it goes into your cash drawer. If it's already there, here is what happens next. *This is general information, not legal advice.*

## If a customer hands you a note that fails your checks

1. **Don't accept it.** Hand it back and ask for another note. Say it didn't pass your check rather than calling it fake; the customer may have received it in change somewhere else.
2. **Check the replacement** the same way.
3. **If it looks deliberate** (several notes, the same person again, or pressure to hurry), call your local police station or 112. Note the time and keep CCTV footage.

## If you find a fake in your cash later

- **Never pass it on**, not even in change. Knowingly using or keeping counterfeit notes is an offence under the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita.
- **Write down what you know**: the day, the till, who may have handed it over.
- **Take it to your bank** and explain where it came from.

## What the bank does (RBI rules)

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Check | Notes paid in at the counter are examined by machine |
| Credit | No credit is given for a counterfeit note |
| Stamp | The note is stamped "Counterfeit Banknote Impounded" and entered in a register |
| Receipt | You get a serially numbered receipt, even if you don't countersign it |
| Return | The note is never returned to you |
| Police | Up to 4 fakes in one transaction: monthly consolidated report to police. 5 or more: sent to police at once with an FIR request |

Banks must also machine-check every note of ₹100 and above before paying it out again. That is one reason most fakes are caught at banks: 97.6% in 2025-26.

## Stop it happening again

- Learn the markings: [how to identify fake notes](/blog/how-to-identify-fake-notes-india/), [₹500 checks](/blog/how-to-check-500-rupee-note-security-features/), [₹200 checks](/blog/how-to-check-200-rupee-note-fake/).
- Put a short checklist next to the till for every cashier.
- If you take a lot of cash, check every note with a machine, the same way banks do. See [machine vs checking by hand](/blog/note-counting-machine-vs-checking-by-hand/).
